# WP4.1 — Console finesse: cutout, glow layers, reflection

Owner: Builder · Status: Briefed · Depends on: WP4 done. Runs alone in the checkout. No fal renders.

Read `CLAUDE.md` first, then `components/console.tsx`.

## Goal

The console currently shows the video and the key/seam states as flat overlays on a photograph. Make them read as part of the object: the video seen through the bezel, and light on metal rather than colour blocks.

## Robin's notes this brief answers

"Create a PNG of the device with a cutout for the screen so the video sits neatly behind it. I don't love the coloured square sitting on the power button, and I'm not sure about the vertical line flashing next to the dials — better to have a glow element as a separate PNG that can fade in and out, also in the reflection."

## 1. Cutout

From `public/console.png`, produce `public/console-cutout.png`: identical, with the screen region made transparent. Use the measured screen bounds (left 0.1317, right 0.6294, top 0.2155, bottom 0.7415) inset by 6–8 px at the source resolution so the black gasket stays on the PNG and its inner shadow falls over the video edge. Do it with a script (`scripts/console-cutout.mts`, sharp or similar) so it can be re-run if the render changes. The video element moves *beneath* the console image in the stacking order; the visible result is unchanged in position but the screen now reads as recessed.

## 2. Key glow layers

Replace the flat colour block on the square key with light. Two routes; do the first, and the second only if Robin supplies the NBP edits.

**Route A (in code):** the key state is a soft-edged square at the key's measured bounds (left 0.643, right 0.673, top 0.679, bottom 0.732), `mix-blend-mode: screen`, with a 2–3 px blur and a faint halo extending ~40% beyond the key, in the ground colour. Below it, a second copy vertically mirrored into the reflection zone at ~35% opacity and 4–6 px blur, positioned by mirroring the key's bounds about the console's base line (measure the base line from the image: the boundary between the feet and their reflection). The listening blink and the rendering colour behave as in WP4.

**Route B (from renders):** if `public/glow/key-{lime,cyan,violet,magenta}.png` are present — NBP edits of the base render with the key lit — extract the difference from the base into transparent glow layers with a script (`scripts/glow-extract.mts`) and cross-fade between them in code. The reflection comes with the render.

## 3. Seam glow

Same treatment as the key for the yellow seam between the modules, replacing the flashing line: a soft layer over the seam's measured bounds in `#FFEE8C`, screen blend, reflection copy; steady when ≥ 2 clips are buffered, a slow 2 s fade in and out while the runway fills. No hard-edged line anywhere.

## 4. Nothing else

Do not touch the ask line, suggestions, strip, screen swap logic or `lib/stream.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `public/console-cutout.png` exists, generated by a committed script; the video renders beneath it and is fully visible with the gasket edge overlapping it by 6–8 px.
2. The key shows no flat colour block: off / listening / rendering read as light on metal, with a visible reflection below the console in the same state.
3. The seam shows no hard-edged line: steady when buffered, slow fade while filling, with reflection.
4. Everything positioned by measured fractions; nothing shifts at 900, 1280 and 1400 px widths.
5. Plain mode is unchanged.
6. Screenshots of theatre mode in each key state are in the handoff.
